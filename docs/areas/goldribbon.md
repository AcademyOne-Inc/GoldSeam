# GoldRibbon — your own record

**What it answers:** what a person has already learned — from their documents and their own words —
and what that learning may be worth as credit at a school they name.

Contract tag on every answer: `goldribbon_v1`.

## The rule that governs this area

**The service keeps nothing.** Everything a person hands it is caller-supplied: it is used to produce
the answer, returned inside a **package your AI holds**, and retained by nothing here. The usage record
logs counts only — never the content, never a name, never a fragment of a document.

There is no account, no login and no stored learner record. If you want the package tomorrow, your AI
keeps it, or you save it yourself.

## The sequence

The tools are meant to be used in order, each adding to the same `package`:

1. **`assemble_my_formal_education(files)`** — transcripts, diplomas, credit-by-exam score reports.
2. **`assemble_my_work_and_credentials(files)`** — a resume, certifications, licenses, military records; `linkedin_url` optional.
3. **`assert_my_life_experiences(entries)`** — what a person has done in their own words. Kept separate from formal education on purpose: an assertion is not a transcript, and the answer never blurs the two.
4. **`my_selfie_ksa_view(package)`** — the **Competency Selfie**: what the evidence shows, placed across the KSA dimensions, every item pointing back at the file it came from.
5. **`draft_my_claims(package)`** — each claim with the **quoted fragment of the person's own evidence** that supports it. A claim whose fragment cannot be found in the evidence is not made.
6. **`suggest_credit_for_my_learning(package, target_unitid)`** — what the learning may earn at a named school, and with a `checklist_id`, line by line against that program's own checklist.

## Assemble, never assess

GoldRibbon **assembles** a person's record. It does not award credit, grade, score or judge. Awarding
is the institution's act, and the answer is built so a person can take it to the office that decides —
with the source of every line attached.

## What it will not do

- It does not store anything, and it cannot retrieve "your last session".
- It does not make a claim that is not quoted from the person's own evidence.
- It does not convert a life experience into a transcript line.
- It does not promise credit. A suggestion is what may be recognised, against what the school published.
